I co-founded Blazer Six in 2014 and served as Product Engineering Lead through its exit in 2025.

Blazer Six built web solutions for desktop, mobile, and anything in between. Under it we created [AudioTheme](/work/audiotheme) and [Cedaro](/work/cedaro), and secured a 10-year partnership with Automattic (WordPress.com) that expanded global distribution.

We launched a suite of commercial web products that served 12k+ customers and generated $2.5M+ in revenue, and automated deployment, versioning, and support workflows across the product lifecycle.
