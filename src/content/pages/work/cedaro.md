Cedaro is a digital product shop from the folks at [Blazer Six](/work/blazer-six). It deals in creative solutions using WordPress. General-purpose themes are available at Creative Market and WordPress.com, but the chief creative outlet is [AudioTheme](/work/audiotheme), a website management solution built for music makers.
