AudioTheme specializes in WordPress music themes and plugins for bands, musicians, and labels. Its plugin is an all-in-one way for independent artists to manage discography, gigs, videos, and more.

I co-founded and built AudioTheme at [Blazer Six](/work/blazer-six), alongside [Cedaro](/work/cedaro). That work led to a 10-year partnership with Automattic (WordPress.com).
